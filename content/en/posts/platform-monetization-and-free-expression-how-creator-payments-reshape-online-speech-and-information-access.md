description: 'We are pleased to announce our next Comparative Legislation Roundtable,
  which will explore how platform-facilitated payments to creators are reshaping online
  expression and access to information. The session will be led by Victoire Rio of
  WHAT TO FIX, who will examine recent developments in creator monetization products,
  policies, transparency, and emerging approaches to regulatory oversight. Matías
  González Mama, Regional Area Coordinator for Latin America, will moderate the discussion.
  The discussion will consider: * How platforms’ monetization and demonetization decisions
  affect freedom of expression and access to information * How existing and emerging
  EU legal frameworks apply to creator monetization * Whether—and to what extent—intermediary
  liability exemptions extend to platforms’ monetization activities * What greater
  transparency and accountability could look like in practice You can read more about
  WHAT TO FIX’s research on monetization below: * Social Media Monetization Systems:
  A Risk Factor * De-Risking Social Media Monetization: an Audit of VLOPs'' Year 3
  DSA Risk Assessments * How Platforms Are Failing Creators Under the DSA * Deep Dive:
  Meta’s Enforcement of Its Monetization Terms and Policies For more on monetization,
